straight into Static balance hold — no rest
Straight into the balance hold, no rest between them. Fatigued plantarflexors blunt the ankle strategy — the small, continuous soleus and gastrocnemius corrections that hold quiet stance — so the room is pushed toward the hip strategy and the single-leg work gets genuinely harder than it would be fresh. That is the point. It also looks more like the moment people actually lose their footing, which is late in the day and tired, not fresh and warm.
